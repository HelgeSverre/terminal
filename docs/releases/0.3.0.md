This release makes output several times faster, adds a find bar and pane
colors, and makes the app start and quit quickly.

- Text output is written straight into compact rows, and emoji sequences,
  flags and accented letters are printed whole. PTY reads are gathered into
  larger deliveries without delaying a single echo.
- Find is a bar docked to the top of the pane, with next and previous match
  and every match highlighted.
- Panes have a color, chosen from a popover in the pane header, and a default
  color in Settings. Pane titles are centered, with a close button; a
  middle-click on the header closes the pane too.
- The login shell starts with the app instead of after its first window, and
  the first frame arrives with the window.
- Quitting no longer leaves the app in the Dock for three seconds.
- The click that brings a window forward now focuses the pane under it.

On one Mac, output throughput in the app went from 100, 118, 45 and 20 MiB/s
in 0.2.4 to 219, 252, 201 and 142 MiB/s for short ASCII lines, long ASCII
lines, truecolor and mixed Unicode. Ghostty measured 280, 288, 201 and 186 in
the same run. An 8.16 GB SQL dump through `cat`, with the window moving and
resizing, took 39 seconds against 150. A warm launch shows its window in about
111 ms and a quit removes the app in about 18 ms. These are measurements on one Mac, not
universal speed guarantees. See the
[method and raw results](https://github.com/HelgeSverre/terminal/blob/main/reports/release-0.3.0-2026-11-11/README.md).

Download the signed, notarized Apple Silicon app ZIP or installer PKG below.
Requires macOS 15 or later.

```shell
brew install --cask helgesverre/tap/helge-terminal
```

For an existing Homebrew installation:

```shell
brew update
brew upgrade --cask helge-terminal
```
