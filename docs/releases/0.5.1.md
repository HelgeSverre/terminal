This release is for developers who embed the terminal in their own app.
The app itself behaves as in 0.5.0.

- The terminal view now starts on Intel Macs. It used to stop while creating
  its first glyph page, because Metal on a Mac whose GPU has its own memory
  refuses the kind of texture storage the view asked for.
- The Swift package's minimum is macOS 14 instead of macOS 15.

The app is still built for Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later.

Installed copies of 0.4.0 or later offer this update at launch or from
Check for Updates… in the app menu.
